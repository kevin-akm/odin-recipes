Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft, fluffy cakes made from a simple batter and cooked on a hot pan.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add the milk and eggs and mix until combined.
  3. Heat a pan and add some butter.
  4. Pour some batter into the pan.
  5. Cook both sides until golden brown.
  6. Serve the pancakes with your preferred toppings.
