Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and a creamy filling.

The layers are baked together until the cheese is melted and the top is golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Prepare the meat sauce with the ground beef, onion, garlic, and tomato sauce.
  3. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  5. Bake until the cheese is melted and golden.
  6. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving.
