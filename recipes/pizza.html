Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a baked dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and toppings.
  4. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  5. Let the pizza cool slightly before serving.
